Retain Event Planning as Cue App's active next roadmap increment.

This continues the existing roadmap direction; it does not replace the roadmap. The earlier roadmap placed Invite & RSVP first, but subsequent delivery established guest records, RSVP states, audience segmentation, and communications capabilities. Those foundations reduce the urgency of completing the standalone Invite & RSVP flow before planning.

Event Planning creates greater customer and platform value now because it closes the largest remaining event-lifecycle gap; establishes the foundation for Sessions, Speakers, Venues, Sponsors, Budgets, Registration, and Analytics; strengthens Cue's differentiation as an event operating system rather than another invitation tool; and extends the existing event workspace and create-event foundation without rebuilding them.

Product boundaries:

- Extend the existing event model and completed event workspace.
- Do not redesign completed modules.
- Begin with an ongoing planning workspace and operational-readiness model.
- Support multi-day structure, ownership, milestones, responsibilities, capacity, templates, and lightweight venue planning.
- Do not absorb full Registration, Session Management, Speaker Management, Budgeting, or detailed Venue Management into this increment.
- AI may suggest plans, milestones, and risks, but users must review and approve changes.

Tradeoff accepted: a complete public invitation, registration, and RSVP journey remains unfinished. Keep Invite & RSVP as the next high-priority lifecycle gap after the Event Planning foundation unless new customer evidence changes the order.

MondayOS should record this decision, associate Event Planning with the appropriate existing epic or task, and surface unresolved product questions before translating it into execution work.
