The Cue Event data model uses capacity, confirmedGuests, and invitedGuests to represent RSVP/attendance — deliberately NOT ticketsSold or any paid-ticketing concept. During TASK-0032 the CPO stage suggested a 'ticketsSold' field, but DEC-0003 (Scope Guardrails) forbids payments/ticketing in the MVP. The dashboard fill bar therefore shows confirmed-of-capacity, a portfolio-aware event-ops signal, not sales. Any future ticketing must first amend DEC-0003. Component: cue-app.
