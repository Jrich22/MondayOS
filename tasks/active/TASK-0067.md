acceptance_criteria:
- 'Generation is project-aware: brand voice, guidelines, campaign objective, audience, themes, restricted subjects/claims,
  approved assets, and platform conventions are all inputs. A test asserts a generation request omitting brand context is
  refused rather than falling back to a generic template.'
- 'Platform adaptation is explicit: one campaign idea produces DISTINCT per-platform variants sharing a variant_group_id,
  never one caption copied across platforms. A test asserts two variants of one idea differ.'
- Supported outputs cover LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, X post/thread, TikTok script, YouTube short script, carousel outline,
  blog, newsletter, product announcement, educational, launch campaign, community post, partner outreach, SEO article.
- Generated content enters as DRAFT and is subject to the existing lifecycle. No generated item may skip review, tested.
- 'The safety rules are enforced at generation and again at review: no fabricated reviews/testimonials, no fabricated statistics,
  no unsupported product claims, no private information, no impersonation. A test asserts a generation carrying a fabricated
  statistic is flagged and blocked from reaching ready-for-review.'
- Sensitive categories (legal, medical, financial, reputational, PII) escalate to a human rather than auto-approving. Tested.
- WeeklyPackage covers a Monday-Sunday week and carries project, week, campaign objective, audience, theme, proposed posts
  with platform/caption/media/CTA/destination/date-time, warnings, expected conversion goal, and recommendation rationale.
- The weekly package is generated by an on-demand deterministic command. No background scheduler is built in this increment;
  recurring execution is left to MondayOS scheduling.
- 'Owner actions: approve entire week, approve selected posts, request revisions, reject individual posts, change date/time,
  pause project, pause platform, pause all publishing.'
- Approve-the-week approves each post individually against its own fingerprint. A test asserts a week-level approval produces
  N per-item approvals and authorizes no unlisted content.
- Changing a date/time through the package resets that item's approval via the existing fingerprint rule. Tested.
- The approval inbox shows project, campaign, post, platform, account reference placeholder, caption, media, CTA, link,
  publish date/time, warnings, expected conversion goal, and approval state.
